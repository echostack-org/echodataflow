# Pipeline examples

Explore example Echodataflow processing pipelines. Download the [recipes](overview_recipes) from the companion [echodataflow-recipes](https://github.com/echostack-org/echodataflow-recipes) repository, then follow the instructions on the pages below to deploy the pipeline.

- [Detect coastal pelagic species in acoustic data](cps.md): Process incoming echosounder data to detect coastal pelagic species (CPS), such as sardine, anchovy, and herring, in near real time
- [Visualize satellite data with a ship track](spasso.md): Overlay ship track on satellite remote sensing and reanalysis products to support adaptive sampling plans
